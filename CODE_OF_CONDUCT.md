# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

HomeServer is an open-source project intended to be useful, understandable, and reusable. Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively.

Examples of expected behavior:

- be respectful of different experience levels and technical backgrounds;
- provide constructive feedback;
- focus discussions on the project and its technical goals;
- assume good faith when reviewing contributions.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, or publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and discussions that violate this standard. Serious or repeated violations may result in participation being restricted.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and other project spaces maintained by the HomeServer project.
